Good to Know
A few things worth reading before you land.
🚪
Entering bars, clubs and accommodation
All bars, clubs and accommodation are strict on stag dos — we'll need to enter in smaller groups. Don't all pile in at once.
🩳
Boat day & what to wear
The weather is looking good (it has rained non-stop all week) — however it's unlikely you'll be going in the sea on the boat day. We're heading straight out from the boat trip so bring a pair of crappy shorts you don't mind leaving behind. Joe and Roger have their own outfit sorted for the day. Everywhere we're going is casual. Wear trainers, not flip flops.
💸
Money & costs
If everyone's happy, we can sort out who owes what when we get back. We'll cover all meals, accommodation and club entry — so no need to worry about it on the weekend.
🚗
Transport
Get an Uber from the airport — we'll be getting Ubers all weekend. They're dirt cheap in Lisbon.
